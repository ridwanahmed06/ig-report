How to get your Instagram export
  1. Open Download your information in Instagram's Accounts Center (log in if asked).
  2. Choose Download or transfer information, pick your account, then Some of your information.
  3. Tick only Followers and following and continue to Download to device.
  4. Set the date range to All time and the format to JSON, then create the file.
  5. Instagram emails you when it's ready, usually within minutes. Download the .zip and drop it above. No need to unzip it.

These are sample accounts, so the rows don't link anywhere.
